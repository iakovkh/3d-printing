## Modifications

The reference was reoriented, non-uniformly scaled to the approved envelope,
voxel-welded into one printable component, given an integrated stabilizing base
and egg-support rim, and cut with a new 42 mm by 22 mm functional bowl. The
result is a modified derivative, not an unaltered copy of the source scan.
