# Approved concept — v001

Status: APPROVED AS ART DIRECTION

The concept image shown in the project chat is an artistic reference only and is explicitly **not** a technical render.

Required visual features carried into geometry:
- volumetric umbrella canopy with scalloped edge and raised ribs;
- classic J-handle;
- separate top keyring loop;
- large tourist-style `Schietwetter` wordmark;
- simplified Hamburg three-tower symbol above the wordmark;
- dark-blue body, cream wordmark, red Hamburg mark.

Intentionally omitted from v001 because of the 45 mm size and 0.4 mm nozzle:
- small gulls;
- thin waves;
- small anchors and raindrop micro-decoration;
- secondary micro-text.

The final review images must come only from the reopened v001 candidate 3MF.
